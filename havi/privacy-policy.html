1 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Havi Studio (“we”, “us”, or “Havi”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Havi mobile application (the “App”). By downloading or using Havi, you agree to the practices described here. A separate Terms of Service document for the App is also available and may be reviewed separately.

Rule #1 of Havi: if we don’t need it, we don’t collect it. The photos you import, the stickers you cut, and the pages you build stay on your device unless you explicitly export them or share them.

2 What we collect & what we don’t

2.1 Information we store on your device (Havi never sees it)

  • Pages & stickers. Your scrapbook pages, cutout PNGs, sticker names, and draft data are saved in Havi’s local sandbox on your iPhone using on-disk storage.
  • Coin balance. Your remaining Havi coins, including complimentary and purchased balances, are stored locally on the device using a combination of in-app preferences and Apple-provided keychain entries. This data is not synced to any external server operated by us.
  • Preferences. Things like your last-used paper style, editor zoom level, and any “Don’t ask again” choices.

2.2 Information processed through Apple’s systems (not on our servers)

  • In-App Purchases. When you buy a coin pack in the Havi Coin Shop, the transaction is processed by Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple shares a purchase receipt with your device so Havi can restore purchased coin bundles. We do not store credit-card numbers or billing details — ever.
  • App Store analytics. Apple may share aggregated, anonymised metrics with developers (installs, sessions, crash summaries) under App Store Connect. We use these only to fix crashes and improve quality.
  • Exported images. When you save or share a page, the image may flow through standard iOS share extensions or system frameworks. Havi does not upload these images to our own cloud.

2.3 What we definitely do NOT collect

  • Contact lists, phone numbers, email addresses, or social graphs.
  • Precise geolocation or GPS history.
  • Health, biometric, or sensitive demographic data.
  • Keystroke logs, screen recordings, or private content from other apps.
  • Advertising IDs for third-party ad targeting. Havi does not include ad SDKs that collect IDFA for personalized advertising.

3 Coins & purchases

Havi’s coin system unlocks downloads and shares. Coins are digital items managed locally and by Apple’s StoreKit. We do not operate a central wallet or a user login server. There is no Havi account. Your balance lives on your device. The App’s Terms of Service document describes refunds and how failures are handled, and is available separately for review.

4 Sharing & export flows

When you tap Share, Havi renders the page to a temporary PNG file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. From that point, whatever you do with the shared image is governed by the receiving app’s own terms and privacy policy — for example, sharing to Instagram means Meta’s rules apply. Havi has no visibility into how the recipient uses the image.

When you tap Download, Havi writes the rendered page PNG to a local destination of the system’s choosing. After that, the file behaves like any other exported media on your device — only you control where it goes next.

5 Children’s privacy

Havi is a general-audience creative app and is not marketed specifically to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that Havi has accidentally received personal data about a child, please contact us at the email below — we will promptly delete any such material from our possession within a reasonable timeframe.

6 Your privacy rights

Because most of your data lives on your device, the easiest way to access, correct, or delete it is right in the app:

1
Access

Open the book, shelf, or coin shop — everything we know about is already visible to you.

2
Edit

Rename stickers, edit page titles, or rearrange pages directly inside the editor.

3
Delete

To wipe everything, delete Havi from your device. Its sandbox is removed along with the app by iOS.

Residents of the EEA, the UK, California, and other jurisdictions with data protection laws may have additional rights regarding any personal information we actually hold. Right now Havi does not operate an account or cloud backend, so the answer to a typical access request is usually “we don’t hold any personal data on you.” But if you believe otherwise, email us — we will respond promptly.

7 Retention

  • On-device data: we keep nothing. It stays with you until you delete the app or clear its documents.
  • Support correspondence: if you email us, we keep your message and reply for as long as reasonably needed to resolve your question, and no longer than 24 months unless a law requires more.
  • Aggregated App Store analytics: kept under Apple’s standard App Store Connect retention policy, outside our control.

8 Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect whatever little information we handle. The Havi app stores its local data in the standard iOS sandbox with data protection enabled; support emails travel over TLS; and the app ships with no network-backed upload of your pages or stickers. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, however — so please keep your device passcode enabled and your Apple ID secure.
